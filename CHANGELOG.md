# Changelog

All notable changes to Hexventure. The game reads this file too: the version on the title screen opens it.

## 0.3 — 25-09-2026

### Improved
- The tile stack works as in Hexhaven: the number on the stack is how many tiles you have, and each time you choose 1 of 3. Choosing no longer costs extra tiles.
- The stack and the choice show real little 3D tiles: a pile with the next tile on top, and a picture of every option.
- With no tiles left there is nothing to choose and no free spots are shown.
- Build mode uses Hexhaven's camera angle (looking down at about 43°, turned slightly).

## 0.2 — 25-09-2026

### New
- A run: start at your hearth with a stack of tiles (bottom right) and three in hand. Placing tiles builds the world; the further from home, the more dangerous it gets.
- Soulslike combat: stamina, a dodge roll with invulnerability (Shift or right-click), and a healing flask with three charges (Q).
- Four new enemies: the Husk (a slow knight with a heavy overhead swing), the Thornspitter (a rooted plant that spits thorns), the Bat (circles you, then dives) and the Boar (charges and is stunned when it hits something).
- Special tiles: the Shrine (spend embers on more hearts, stamina or damage), the Lair (a miniboss guards a World Fragment) and, after three fragments, the Boss tile with the Hollow King.
- Two minibosses: the Grove Warden and the Slime King, with warning rings where their big attacks land.
- Stairs: a card that makes a hill climbable from the side facing flat land.
- Embers: enemies drop them. When you die you leave them behind; pick them up again before you die a second time.
- Resting at the campfire heals you and refills the flask, but the enemies come back.
- Clearing every enemy from a tile gives you a new tile.
- Changelog on the title screen.

### Improved
- Tiles are held and dropped as in Hexhaven: the tile follows the cursor on a spring, a landing marker ripples under it, and it drops with a bounce and a shockwave.
- Bridges, stairs and lairs only come into your hand when there is a place for them.
- Beaches: the lake bed slopes up to the land, so water tiles blend into one shore.
- A loading screen instead of a blank page with text while the game starts.

## 0.1 — 25-09-2026

### New
- First playable prototype: build hex tiles (meadow, forest, water, hill, bridge), walk through the world, fight slimes, open chests.
- Title screen, autosave and save slots, pause menu and a ready-built debug world.
